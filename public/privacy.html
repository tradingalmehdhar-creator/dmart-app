1. Personal Data We Collect

Information gathered to facilitate grocery delivery and daily needs fulfillment

In accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 of Malaysia (“PDPA”), we collect only the information reasonably necessary to provide fresh grocery shopping, order packing, delivery routing, and customer support.

👤 Identification & Contact
  • Full Name: To identify your order and address you accurately.
  • Phone Number: Primary account identifier used for OTP verification, delivery SMS/calls by riders, and order alerts.
  • Email Address: Used for account receipts, order invoices, and service correspondence.
🏠 Delivery & Physical Address
  • Street Address & Unit: House/apartment/building unit, condominium floor, and street name.
  • Postcode, City & State: Malaysian geographic territory (e.g. Kuala Lumpur, Selangor).
  • Delivery Notes: Instructions provided for safe drop-off (e.g., “Leave at guardhouse”).
📍 Geolocation Information
  • Delivery Pin Coordinates (GPS): Latitude and longitude to enable on-time grocery dispatch.
  • Real-time Delivery Tracking: Used during active order delivery to route fleet riders to your doorstep.
🛒 Transaction & Purchase History
  • Purchased SKUs: Items ordered, quantities, prices, discounts, and promotions redeemed.
  • Order Metadata: Order timestamps, invoice numbers, fulfillment status, and receipts.
  • Payment Method: Selected method (COD, FPX, TnG eWallet). We do not store credit card CVV or bank passwords.
Payment Data Notice
All digital payments (FPX Online Banking, Touch 'n Go eWallet, and credit/debit cards) are processed directly by Bank Negara Malaysia (BNM)-compliant payment service providers. D-Mart stores only transaction reference IDs and payment status, never your banking credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

Purposes for processing your personal information

We process your personal information strictly for genuine commercial and operational purposes, including:

  • Fulfillment & Grocery Delivery: Packing your items, verifying stock availability, dispatching to fleet delivery riders, and completing doorstep drop-off.
  • Account Authentication & Security: Generating one-time passcodes (OTP), authenticating phone credentials via Firebase Auth, and preventing unauthorized account takeover.
  • Order Communications: Sending automated notifications regarding order confirmation, packing progress, out-for-delivery status, and digital receipts.
  • Customer Care & Dispute Resolution: Addressing damaged item replacements, refund requests, delivery inquiries, and customer service tickets.
  • Service Enhancement & Analytics: Identifying frequently ordered grocery items, reducing dispatch bottlenecks, improving app speed, and troubleshooting app crashes.
  • Regulatory & Financial Compliance: Maintaining statutory accounting books, SST/tax compliance records, and commercial dispute evidence under Malaysian legal standards.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data to third-party data brokers or unaffiliated advertisers.

4. Location Data Usage & Permissions

How GPS and geolocation information is handled

Because D-Mart is a physical on-demand grocery delivery service, location data is critical for accurate order fulfillment.

  • Customer Location: When you select your delivery address or allow browser/app location permissions, we capture your latitude and longitude coordinates to calculate distance from our local distribution hub, confirm delivery coverage, and ensure the delivery partner navigates to the exact unit.
  • Delivery Partner Location: Fleet riders transmit real-time location data while delivering active orders so you can observe the estimated time of arrival (ETA) on your live tracking screen.
  • Background Location: We do not track customers’ background location when the app is closed. Location is accessed only with your express permission during address selection or order tracking.

You may revoke location permissions at any time via your device or browser operating system settings. Note that disabling location may require manually entering street addresses and postcodes during checkout.

5. Data Storage, Retention & Security Safeguards

Protection measures against unauthorized access and loss

We employ modern security safeguards designed to prevent unauthorized alteration, disclosure, destruction, or misuse of personal data:

🛡️ Encryption in Transit & Rest

All communications between your device and our servers are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Database storage is encrypted at rest using AES-256 standards.

🔑 Access Control (RBAC)

Strict Firebase Security Rules enforce role-based access. Customer data is strictly segregated so that only the authenticated user and authorized operational staff can access specific order records.

⏱️ Data Retention Period

We retain active account data as long as your account is active. Financial invoices and order records are retained for statutory retention periods mandated under Malaysian taxation and accounting regulations (typically up to 7 years).

6. Your Rights Under Malaysian Law (PDPA)

Statutory protections and control over your personal data

Under the Malaysian Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA), you are entitled to exercise the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of the personal information D-Mart holds about you.
  • Right to Correction & Rectification: You may update or correct inaccurate, incomplete, or misleading profile information directly in the app or by notifying our support.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw your consent for receiving marketing push notifications, SMS promotions, or non-essential communications at any time.
  • Right to Prevent Processing for Direct Marketing: You have the absolute right to opt out of promotional broadcasts without affecting your ability to place essential orders.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: You may request permanent deletion of your account and personal records as detailed in Section 7.

7. Account & Data Deletion Request

How to permanently delete your D-Mart account and personal data

We respect your right to digital privacy. You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your D-Mart user account and all personal data associated with your profile at any time.

1
Option A: Direct In-App Request Open the D-Mart app > Navigate to Account Profile / Settings > Select “Delete Account (Permanent)” > Confirm deletion via the confirmation prompt.
2
Option B: Written Request via Support Email Send an email from your registered email address or quote your registered Malaysian mobile number to tradingalmehdhar@gmail.com with the subject line “D-Mart Account and Data Deletion Request”.
3
Verification & Processing Timeline Upon receiving your request, our Data Protection team will verify identity within 48 hours and permanently purge your credentials, saved addresses, profile data, and active records within 14 business days.
⚠️ What Happens Upon Deletion?
Your Firebase Authentication identity will be permanently deleted, active shopping carts removed, and saved delivery addresses unlinked. Please note that historical accounting transaction records (e.g. tax receipts, financial journal entries) are retained in an anonymized format for statutory periods as mandated by Malaysian Inland Revenue Board (LHDN) regulations.

8. Minors & Children’s Privacy

Protection of individuals below legal capacity

D-Mart’s services are intended for adults and individuals with legal capacity to enter into binding purchase contracts under Malaysian law (generally individuals aged 18 and older). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18 years of age.

If you are a parent or guardian and discover that your minor child has provided personal information without your supervision, please contact our support team immediately so we can promptly delete the account.
